    How to get your files
    1. LinkedIn. Me, Settings & Privacy, Data privacy, Get a copy of your data. Choose Connections only, then Request archive. Unzip the email download and import Connections.csv.
    2. Facebook. Settings, Accounts Center, Your information and permissions, Download your information. Pick Friends, format JSON. Import your_friends.json.
    3. Instagram. Same Accounts Center path. Pick Followers and following, format JSON. Import followers_1.json and following.json together. Instagram only gives usernames, so these people match your LinkedIn contacts only when their username is their full name.
    4. Everyone else (X, Threads, alumni groups, people you know offline). Make a spreadsheet with columns Name, Company, Title, Platform, save it as CSV, and import it. X's own export has account numbers only, no names, so use the spreadsheet for X.
